Privacy Policy

Dice Roller · Effective date: 12 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Dice Roller mobile application ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use it. The App is published by Shurutech and is available on Google Play.

Short version: The App does not ask you to create an account, does not collect your name, email, phone number, location, or contacts, and does not store any personal data on our servers. The only third-party service we integrate is Google AdMob, which serves a banner advertisement and may collect limited device and advertising identifiers as described below.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information from you. All app preferences (such as theme, selected dice type, and roll history) are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may automatically collect and process information including but not limited to:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (AAID on Android)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system, and language
  • Anonymous interaction data with the ads (such as impressions and clicks)

The App requests non-personalized ads by default, meaning AdMob is instructed not to use behavioural targeting. Even so, Google may process the data described above as a data controller. Google's use of this data is governed by its own privacy policy:

You can reset or limit your Android advertising identifier at any time through your device settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads.

3. Permissions

The App requests only the following Android permissions:

  • Internet — required to load advertisements via AdMob.
  • Vibrate — used for haptic feedback when rolling dice.

The App does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, location, or any other sensitive permission.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because the App does not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no user data for us to retain or delete. Any locally stored preferences and roll history are deleted automatically when you uninstall the App or clear its data from your device settings.

6. Security

No transmission over the Internet is fully secure. We take reasonable measures to ensure that ad network connections use encrypted transport (HTTPS), but we cannot guarantee absolute security of any data processed by third parties.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Effective date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
